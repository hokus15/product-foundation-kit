# Product opportunity

Record a need or hypothesis to investigate and prioritize.

## Problem and affected people

Describe the situation and its consequence.

## Evidence and current alternatives

Link available observations and distinguish hypotheses from facts. Describe current alternatives only when current practice affects the problem, scope, or expected improvement.

## Desired outcome

Describe the valuable change and how it could be observed.

## Questions and next action

Identify what remains unknown and which decision the answer will inform.
